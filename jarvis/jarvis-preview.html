        Rules & regulations

        What the Wharton Global High School Investment Competition 2026 to 2027 requires, researched from the official Wharton Global Youth Program website and competition portal on 25 September 2026.

        Your SurveyMonkey Apply packet is the final word. Wharton released the detailed trading requirements, deliverable instructions and evaluation criteria to team leaders on 15 September. If anything below differs from that packet, follow the packet. Rules marked Unverified came from a third-party summary and need checking.

        Check these first

        1. When does trading end? The official competition portal lists "November 6: Investment Policy Statement Due and Trading Ends". Your daily briefing and the Wharton FAQ describe trading running to December 4. Open the Trading Details page in SurveyMonkey Apply and confirm, because the whole trading plan depends on it.
        2. Starting cash. A third-party summary says each team starts with $500,000 of virtual cash. Your StockTrak account shows N/A. Make sure you are looking at the official competition account, not a practice one.
        3. Minimum trade. The same third-party summary says teams must make at least one trade by 10 October 2026 at 4:00 p.m. ET to stay eligible for the semifinals and Global Finale. Treat this as real until the packet says otherwise.

        Key dates

        How you are judged

        Official

        Success is not decided by portfolio performance. Wharton says portfolio growth has "little to do with the final outcome".

        Official

        Teams are judged on the quality of their investment strategy, how well it fits the client's objectives, the strength of their research and how clearly they communicate it.

        Official

        The top 50 teams become semifinalists based on the strength of their Investment Policy Statement and Final Report. Semifinals are virtual presentations.

        Official

        The top 10 teams go to the Global Finale at Wharton in Philadelphia (in person or virtual). Every team member must take part in the live presentation. No individual feedback is given.

        Deliverables

        Official team roster: due 9 October 2026, 5:00 p.m. ET. No members can be added after it is submitted.

        Semifinalists must also send a letter on official school letterhead confirming the school allows the team to take part and that all students and the advisor belong to the school.

        Trading and the account

        Official

        Each team shares one Wharton Investment Simulator (WInS) account. All trading happens there.

        Official

        Practice period ran 15 to 25 September 2026. Official trading begins 28 September 2026.

        Unverified

        At least one trade by 10 October 2026, 4:00 p.m. ET, or the team cannot advance.

        Unverified

        Up to 200 trades in total. Each buy or sell counts as one trade.

        Unverified

        Allowed: cash, stocks priced at $5 or more, approved ETFs (at least one ETF is required), and US Treasury bonds (optional).

        Unverified

        Commissions: $25 per stock trade and $10 per bond trade. Order size is also limited relative to a stock's daily trading volume.

        Unverified rules come from a third-party summary (Aralia Education), not from Wharton. Confirm them in the Trading Details page of your SurveyMonkey Apply packet.

        Team and eligibility

        • 4 to 6 students per team, including the team leader, all from the same high school. Falling below 4 or going above 6 means disqualification.
        • No student can be on more than one team.
        • The team leader must be at least 16 on 28 September 2026. They are the main contact and handle all submissions. The team leader cannot change after 28 September without approval.
        • Members can be added or removed until the roster is submitted. After that, no additions, and removals need written advisor approval.
        • Students must be current high school students without a diploma. Home-schooled students can join with documentation.
        • The team name must not be offensive. Wharton can require a change.

        Advisors

        • One primary advisor is required: a teacher or educator at your school. An advisor can have at most 5 teams.
        • The advisor guides and monitors but cannot make decisions or take part in trading or strategy.
        • Secondary advisors (a parent or finance professional) are allowed.
        • No paid advisors, education consultants or paid agents of any kind, and no non-Wharton courses that claim to teach the competition. This means disqualification, and Wharton may use investigators to check.

        AI use and academic integrity

        This applies to Jarvis and your daily briefing. Wharton allows generative AI for brainstorming and idea generation only. You cannot submit AI work as your own, and any AI-generated material must be cited like any other source. Use Jarvis and the report paragraphs to think, then write the final text yourselves.
        • Read the Wharton Global Youth Program Statement of AI Use before submitting written work.
        • Follow the University of Pennsylvania Code of Academic Integrity and review the CFA Institute Asset Manager Code.
        • No plagiarism, including from advisors. No made-up analyses, teamwork stories or investment results.
        • Cite every source, and credit images or media you did not create.

        What gets a team disqualified

        • Contacting the competition client.
        • Using paid advisors or education consultants.
        • Plagiarism or academic dishonesty.
        • Going below 4 or above 6 members.
        • Removing members or changing advisors without approval.
        • Students registering themselves instead of through the advisor.
        • Refusing to change an offensive team name.
        • Enrolling in competing non-Wharton courses.
        • Unlawful discrimination.
        • Negative, threatening or offensive behaviour, online or in person.

        Semifinals, finale and prizes

        • No recording, screenshots, photos or posting of semifinal or finale presentations. Doing so means dismissal.
        • Semifinalists and finalists sign a consent and liability waiver. Travel is paid by the student or school, and at least one adult must go with an in-person team. Virtual teams provide their own equipment and internet.
        • All eligible teams: a digital participation credential in February 2027. Semifinalists and finalists: a certificate of achievement.
        • Global Finalists (top 10): each student gets the fee waived for the "Understanding Your Money" online course. Runners-up get 50% off one summer online program.
        • Global Champion: each student gets one free online summer program, and the school gets one team fast-tracked to the 2027 to 2028 semifinals.
