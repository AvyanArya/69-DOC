The Salon

Beauty isn't just a service.
It's an experience.

Eikon was built around a simple idea — an hour in the chair should feel like an hour away from the city. Soft light, unhurried hands, and a team that listens before it lifts a pair of scissors.

Our artists stay current with the techniques that matter: nanoplastia and keratin smoothing, balayage and lived-in colour, hydra facials, and bridal looks built to hold through a long, warm Indian evening.

  • Consultation before every service — no surprises, no upselling
  • Vegan, ammonia-free formulations across colour and treatments
  • Sanitised tools and single-use disposables for every guest
  • Separate quiet rooms for bridal and spa appointments
