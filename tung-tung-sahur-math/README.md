# Tung Tung Tung Sahur — Math Adventure

A single-file, fully brown math game for young kids. Open `index.html` in any
browser — no build step, no dependencies, no network calls.

## The game

- Single-digit **addition**, **subtraction** and **multiplication**, four big tap targets each.
- Subtraction never goes negative; multiple-choice distractors are always whole and non-negative.
- Counting dots under every question. For `6 × 7` they lay out as a real 6 × 7 rectangle,
  which is how the times tables are meant to be seen.
- 10 rounds, a streak bonus, stars, and a 1–3 star result screen.
- Any operation can be switched off (never all three), and sound can be muted.
- Keyboard `1`–`4` also picks an answer.

## Tung Tung Tung Sahur

He celebrates when you are right — jumps, swings his bat, waves, kicks his legs,
sparkles, and sets off confetti. He is genuinely sad when you are wrong: brows
droop, he slumps, the bat lowers, and he cries. Then he tells you the right
answer so the round still teaches something. Between questions he sways, blinks,
sweats a little, and his eyes follow your pointer.

## About the character

Tung Tung Tung Sahur started as an Indonesian TikTok by **@noxaasht** (28 Feb 2025)
and was swept into the "Italian brainrot" canon despite being entirely Indonesian
in origin. The name imitates a *kentongan* — a hollow wooden slit-drum struck
before dawn in Indonesian villages to wake neighbours for *sahur*, the pre-dawn
meal during Ramadan. That is also why the correct-answer sound is a wooden knock.

**All artwork in this game is original**, drawn as inline SVG so every part of him
animates separately. No meme images are copied or hotlinked.

## Notes

- Works down to a 360 × 640 phone with the question and all four answers above the fold.
- Sound is synthesised with the Web Audio API; nothing is downloaded.
- Honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.
