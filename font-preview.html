Pick the secondary face.

Fraunces is fixed — it carries the wordmark, every heading and the long sub-copy, and it is not changing. The choice below is only the secondary face: the one doing the unglamorous work of labels, numbers, buttons, chips and 10–13px captions, where a serif turns to mush. Each column is the same slice of real Lumera UI, so compare the small text, not the headings.

How to choose: squint at the SAVINGS RATE labels and the numbers, not the headline. The winner is whichever stays crisp and even at 10–11px while still feeling like it belongs beside Fraunces. Tell me the name and I will swap it in — it is one line in the font stack, so switching later is cheap.
