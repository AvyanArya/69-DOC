**public-domain U.S. Government work**, or **made for this project**. Nothing was generated

## 4 · Stylised recreations (NOT real newspapers)

The eight broadsheet front pages in the opening — *The Financial Record, The Market Courier,
The Capital Dispatch, The World Ledger, Metro Journal, The Daily Broadsheet, The Republic Gazette,
City & Finance* — are **designed props**. Front pages of real newspapers from September 2008
are under copyright and could not be used.

* The mastheads are invented and every page is marked **"ILLUSTRATIVE EDITION"** in its folio line.
* The headlines and body copy describe real events and use figures that can be checked
  (Lehman's Chapter 11 filing with $639 bn in assets and $613 bn in debts; Bank of America's ≈$50 bn
  purchase of Merrill Lynch; the Dow's 504-point fall on 15 Sep and 777-point fall on 29 Sep 2008;
  the Fed's $85 bn loan to AIG; the $700 bn rescue plan).
* The photographs printed on them are the genuine archival pictures listed above, with their credits
  in the captions.

The circled hero headline "LEHMAN BROTHERS FILES FOR BANKRUPTCY" is on one of these recreations.
The genuine page carrying the same headline (Wikinews, 17 Sep 2008) lies next to it in the pile
and returns in close-up, credited as genuine, in Scene 5.

## 7 · Made for this project

* **Narration** – synthesised with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) (Apache-2.0), voice `am_michael`.
* **Score** – composed in `scripts/build_audio.py`, performed by FluidSynth with the FluidR3 GM SoundFont (MIT licence).
* **Sound effects** – synthesised from scratch in `scripts/audio_lib.py`.
* **Paper textures, halftones, globe, charts, typography animation** – generated by the code in this folder.
* **Depth maps** – [Depth Anything V2 Small](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Small) (Apache-2.0).
* **World map** – Natural Earth 1:110m land (public domain) via `world-atlas`.
* **Fonts** (all SIL Open Font Licence or Apache-2.0, in `public/fonts`): Playfair Display, Newsreader, Inter, Oswald, Anton, JetBrains Mono, Special Elite, Permanent Marker, UnifrakturCook.
* **Rendering** – [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev) (free for individuals and small teams; companies need a licence), FFmpeg.
